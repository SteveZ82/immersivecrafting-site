Pair your device

Open the app on iPhone, iPad or Vision Pro. Your Mac appears by itself: tap Pair and confirm the six-digit code on the Mac.

Q&A

Do I need to open ports on my router?

No. The automatic connection crosses the router by itself. Only the strictest networks need a guided rule or a VPN such as Tailscale, and the app walks you through it.2

Do I need an account?

MAControl has no account or password of its own. Away from home it uses your iCloud, where your Mac’s address is stored encrypted end to end.8

Does it work on cellular?

Yes, even on 5G. Quality adapts to the bandwidth available while you work.6

What if my Mac is asleep or locked?

A sleeping Mac can’t be reached from outside, so leave it on, even with the display off. If it’s locked, you can unlock it from the app: the password travels encrypted and is never stored.

Which languages does it speak?

English, Italian, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and Simplified Chinese. MAControl uses your device’s language automatically.

What does “lifetime support” mean?

You pay once for the client. There are no in-app purchases and no ads: the app is yours forever, and future updates and support are included, with no subscription.7
